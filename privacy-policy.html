Privacy Policy for Tappy Cat

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Tappy Cat ("the app") is developed and published by Wizard Viking Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Information we collect ourselves

None. Tappy Cat has no accounts of its own and no servers of our own. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location. The Google services described below handle the data they need on Google's servers; we see only the summaries they provide.

Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and where players get stuck, the app sends anonymous usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase — for example that a level was started or finished and with what score, that a daily reward was claimed, or that a shop item was bought with in-game currency. Firebase also collects an app-instance ID, the advertising ID and technical device information (device model, operating system version, coarse country/region). We see these only as aggregated reports. Google's handling of this data is described at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Google Play Games (optional)

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the app sends your achievements and your best scores and endless-run distances to Google so they appear in your Play Games profile and on the leaderboards, where other players can see your Play Games gamer name and score. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; you can manage your Play Games profile and visibility in the Google Play Games app. The game works fully without signing in.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a reminder on your device for when your daily reward is ready. The reminder is created on the device itself; no server sends it and no data leaves your device for it. You can turn it off in your device's notification settings.

Information stored on your device

Your progress — levels completed, coins, tickets, unlocked cats, trails and settings — is saved only in the app's own storage on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. To serve and measure those ads, and to detect invalid traffic and fraud, AdMob collects your device's advertising ID and technical device information (such as device model, operating system version, coarse country/region and IP address). We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you; we only see aggregate earnings reports.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Settings → Google → Ads on your Android device. Google's own handling of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app asks for your consent before serving personalised ads, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change that choice later from the app's settings.

Purchases

Any in-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Payment details are handled entirely by Google; we never see or store your card, bank or billing information. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase completed.

Children

Tappy Cat is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

Data retention and deletion

Because we hold no personal data on any server of our own, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Google deletes event-level Firebase Analytics data after two months (the default retention period). Play Games achievements and scores can be removed by deleting your Play Games profile in the Google Play Games app. To remove everything the app has stored, uninstall Tappy Cat or clear its data from your device's app settings. To have advertising data associated with your advertising ID removed, reset the advertising ID as described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this address with a new "last updated" date.
